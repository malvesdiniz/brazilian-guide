Hi! If you received this link, it means you were invited to the wedding of the two most beautiful people in the world: Mariana and Sam 😌

The wedding is happening in Fortaleza, Brazil, and we know that for many of you this is a pretty long trip. So we completely understand that you may not want to fly all the way to Brazil just to spend a few days in Fortaleza — even though Fortaleza is amazing and we love it very much.

The good news is that this gives you the perfect excuse to turn the wedding trip into a proper holiday. Some of you may want beaches and relaxation, others may prefer cities, nature, culture or adventure. Some of you are traveling with children, some are not. Everyone has a different travel style, so there is no single itinerary that makes sense for everybody.

That is exactly why we created this guide. Instead of trying to plan everyone’s trip individually, we wanted to put together a place where you can explore different options and figure out what makes sense for you.

You can use it to discover places around Brazil, explore the coast of Ceará, or simply find ideas for what to do in and around Fortaleza. This can also help you decide how to organize your flights — whether you want to visit another part of Brazil before the wedding, continue traveling afterwards, or simply fly in and out of Fortaleza and organize domestic flights separately.

For now, the main goal is to help you answer the most important question first: where do you actually want to go? Once you know that, planning flights and the rest of the trip becomes much easier.

We will keep adding information to this guide over time, especially when we notice that the same questions keep coming up. So think of this as a living guide rather than a finished travel website. If there is something you think would be useful to add, suggestions are very welcome!

And of course, you can still message us if you have questions. We really want to make this trip as easy and enjoyable as possible for everyone — especially because, closer to the wedding, we may be slightly less efficient human beings 😅

We hope this helps you plan an amazing trip, and we cannot wait to have you in Brazil with us.

P.S.: This is not the wedding website. That one will come later, together with the official invitation.
